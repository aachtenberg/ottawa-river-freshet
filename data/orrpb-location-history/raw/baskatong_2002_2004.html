About this data

Water level is measured in metres according to the geodetic reference above mean sea level and is either the level measured at 24:00 (midnight) or the daily average.

Daily average flow is measured in cubic metres per second (m3/s).

The median is the middle value of the daily average flows or water levels for the 30-year period from 1991 to 2020 and corresponds to the 50th percentile. For example, if the median is 100 m³/s, this means that half of the flows observed for a given day during this period were higher than 100 m³/s and half were lower.

The normal range, identified with the light green band on the figure, is where observed levels or flows occur 80% of the time.

Hourly level and flow data values are the instantaneous measurement for the given time.

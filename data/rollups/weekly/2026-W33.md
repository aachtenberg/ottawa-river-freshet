### HQ-1 · Des Joachims 4-check guardrail failure (Aug 14) — HIGH PRIORITY

**What happened:** Prov 587 m³/s cleared all four sanity checks on Aug 14. Final was 388 m³/s (−34%, implied last-2h mean −1,801 m³/s → ARTIFACT by feasibility back-out). Brief logged "all 4 checks PASS, credible." Corrected next day.

**Root cause:** Two compounding factors: (1) The Aug 9 Des Joachims "confirmed" baseline used in mass-balance and pool-consistency checks was 733 m³/s at check time — that value was itself incorrect (later corrected to 419 m³/s). The inflated baseline made 587 look plausible. (2) Des Joachims partial-day running means captured at ~22:00 UTC systematically overestimate because afternoon high-flow readings dominate the mean; the feasibility back-out against the *provisional* (not final) appears to pass because there are many hours yet to absorb the overshoot.

**Recommendation:** (a) Add a Des Joachims-specific provisional discount factor (~0.75×) when the running mean is captured before 22:00 UTC. (b) Do not use a "confirmed" value as a check baseline until it is ≥ 48 h old. (c) Add a flag when any check input itself has a correction-pending status.

### HQ-3 · ORRPB retroactive revision policy

**What happened:** Between the Aug 9 and Aug 10 brief cycles, ORRPB revised Aug 8 finalized flows upward (+274 m³/s Des Joachims, +267 m³/s Otto Holden, +40 m³/s Témiscaming). These are values that appeared as "confirmed" in the Aug 9 brief but changed in the DB the following day.

**Implication:** ORRPB daily flows should be treated as preliminary until ≥ 48–72 h have elapsed. The current brief convention marks a value as "confirmed" on the day after provisional. This may overstate certainty.

**Recommendation:** Consider a "preliminary-final" state (1 day after provisional) distinct from "confirmed" (48+ h, no subsequent revision). Update claim-ledger verdict criteria accordingly.

### HQ-5 · Proxy stale-provisional persistence

**What happened:** After ORRPB corrected Aug 13 and Aug 14 Des Joachims flows (finalized values 431 and 388 m³/s respectively), the proxy temporarily returned stale provisional values (673 and 652 m³/s) for those dates. The proxy stores a running mean at ~22:00 UTC and does not reliably re-ingest finalized values when ORRPB updates them retroactively.

**Recommendation:** Implement a cluster-side backfill job that, for each date in `orrpb_river_flows`, re-fetches the ORRPB finalized discharge once per day for up to 5 days after the original date. This would eliminate stale-provisional artifacts in the DB and prevent them from propagating into check baselines (see HQ-1).

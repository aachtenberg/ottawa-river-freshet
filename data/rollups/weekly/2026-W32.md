## §2 Notable Events

### 2.1 Aug 5 False Surge Alarm (CRITICAL — Corrected)

The Aug 5 brief headlined a ⚠⚠⚠ extreme surge: Des Joachims at 995 m³/s (+164% DoD) and Otto Holden at 740 m³/s (+113%), sourced from ORRPB provisional flows. A forward-looking property-risk forecast was issued ("wave in transit, estimated arrival 1–3 days downstream").

ORRPB-finalized values: Des Joachims = **474 m³/s**, Otto Holden = **356 m³/s**. The provisional figures were infeasible — a 995 m³/s release at Des Joachims with a falling Bryson headpond (which must drain to supply it) and no anomaly at either Bryson release gauges or downstream Chenaux violates mass balance.

The Aug 6 brief led with an explicit **CORRECTION**, retracted the property-risk forecast, and credited the revision to ORRPB finalization. No downstream impact occurred. Resolution was complete within ≤24 hours.

**Root cause:** ORRPB afternoon provisional artifacts at Des Joachims — a systematic pattern observed 5 of 7 days this window (Aug 4–9). The provisional exceeds finalized by 30–100%+ when ORRPB updates mid-afternoon before full daily-average computation is complete.

**Protocol change:** The `freshet-daily-brief` routine was updated (commit 2026-08-06T10:35 EDT, author: aachten) to require a 4-part provisional-flow sanity check before headlining any ORRPB current-day flow figure:
1. Feasibility back-out (does upstream storage support the claimed release?)
2. Mass balance (flow in ≈ flow out ± Δstorage)
3. Pool consistency (headpond trend vs release rate)
4. Downstream response (does next-station gauge confirm the wave?)

This guardrail was applied correctly on Aug 6–9: all Des Joachims provisional artifacts were quarantined to data tables only, never headlined.

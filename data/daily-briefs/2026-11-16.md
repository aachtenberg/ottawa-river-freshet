## In plain language

### Upstream — the upper basin

The outflow from Lake Timiskaming dropped back to 366 cubic metres per second on November 16, down from 401 the day before and 16 lower than a week earlier. That starts a new run of days below 400. It is 198 days since the May 2 peak of 2,741.

The Mattawa gauge fell back 28 centimetres, undoing the previous day's jump and leaving it only four centimetres above its level of a week earlier. Pembroke was essentially unchanged. The fourteen storage reservoirs were quiet: 12 steady, Lake Timiskaming down three centimetres and Poisson Blanc up three.

### At the property — Lac Coulonge / Mansfield

The lake gauge at Fort-Coulonge read 106.042 metres at 9 PM UTC on November 16, half a centimetre lower than the day before. It is 96 centimetres below the first alert level and 146 centimetres below the minor-flood level. The river board's November 10 outlook still applied, with the next one due on November 17.

Well downstream, the Bryson Generating Station again ran unchanged: about 372 cubic metres per second in total, a fifth of it over the spillway. The dam pond eased three centimetres and remains about 88 centimetres above its normal operating ceiling. Carillon, the last dam in the basin, was steady.

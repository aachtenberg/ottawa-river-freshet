## Notes

- **Upper cascade dispatch uptick:** Première-Chute, Quinze, and Îles all increased by 5–9% today. This likely reflects a daytime dispatch ramp (or a change in the reservoir gate positions) rather than an inflow-driven event — the Témiscaming outflow is flat at 340 m³/s and Mattawa dropped 9 cm, so there's no fresh inflow signal from the upper basin. The increase is consistent with peaking/generation demand variability. Watch tomorrow's readings to determine if this is sustained or a one-day dispatch event.

- **Bryson headpond stall:** The pond has held exactly at 105.37 m for two consecutive days (Jul23 and Jul24 both showing 105.37 m at their T19 readings). The three-day drift up (+2 cm each on Jul21→22→23) has paused — but so has the drawdown that began July 20. With spill still declining, the headpond is receiving less outflow pressure while turbine throughput holds steady. If the stall continues and spill keeps dropping, the headpond could drift upward again; if operators hold turbines flat and continue reducing spill, the pond may eventually settle.

## Notes

- **Why is the Bryson headpond rising while release is falling?** With the upper cascade (Première-Chute, Îles) rebounding 30–43% today, significantly more water is arriving at the Bryson pond. The total release fell 20% — mostly by cutting spillway flow — but the rebound in upstream inflow exceeded the reduction in spill, so the net was accumulation: +15 cm. The operators appear to be running a tight turbine setpoint (~289 m³/s constant) and using the spillway as a regulating valve. As the headpond approaches the June 21st all-time high, it will be worth watching whether they increase spill again or continue to let the pond stay elevated.

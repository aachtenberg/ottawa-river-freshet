The operators are clearly aware of this and appear to be in emergency drawdown mode, but the headpond is still gaining water.

At the basin's terminal dam at Carillon, 300 kilometres southeast, operators reduced releases by about 200 cubic metres per second today, to roughly 1,778 cubic metres per second through the turbines with no spillway use.

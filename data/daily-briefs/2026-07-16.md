The upper basin is simply in steady late-season baseflow.

### At the property — Lac Coulonge / Mansfield

The Vigilance gauge at Fort-Coulonge read 106.09 metres at 4 PM today, essentially unchanged from yesterday afternoon's 106.10 metres (down 0.4 cm). That confirms that yesterday's 8-centimetre rise was a one-day event — likely a local rain pulse — now absorbed. The lake is holding steady well within normal summer range, approximately 91 centimetres below the level at which any informal advisory would begin. This is the same kind of quiet typical of mid-July.

Several kilometres downstream of the property — reached by following the south end of the lake as it narrows and winds down to the Bryson Generating Station — a notable operational shift occurred today. This is a major change in operating posture — the shift from spillway-heavy to turbine-heavy signals that less water needs to be discharged at high rate, consistent with the reductions in flow arriving from upstream stations. The dam pond at Bryson rose 4 centimetres to 105.32 metres, remaining 65 centimetres above its standard operating ceiling — the 49th consecutive day above that ceiling.

## Conventions

- **Filename**: `YYYY-MM-DD_short_slug.md`. Date is the compile date, not the date being analyzed.
- **No em-dashes** in notes that get shared publicly (FB, community responses). Standard prose elsewhere.
- **Plain-language preface first** on every note, then numbers and tables, then methodology and caveats, then related material. See `feedback_doc_plain_language_preface` in the user's auto-memory for the convention.
- **Cite ORRPB / Vigilance / HQ / WSC sources** with a footnote-style block at the bottom of every note that uses their data. Per ORRPB's terms, present derived statistics, not the raw daily tables.

## Figures

All figures live in `figures/`. Per-note convention:

- Reference figures by `figures/NAME.png` (relative) inside community notes
